\begin{frame}{The proposed second layer}
\Large Turn \textcolor{violet}{explicitly selected} repeated agent workflows into evaluated, versioned program candidates.\vspace{1em}
\begin{block}{Hypothesis}A narrower DSPy program on a smaller model may reduce compute cost while maintaining or improving task quality. This must be tested, not assumed.\end{block}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{The thesis needs boundaries}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1em}
\item The claim that all work can be reduced to agent-controlled compute is a research thesis, not established fact.
\item Human judgment, relationships, physical work, legal duties, and consent still matter.
\item No workflow or conversation enters the proposed pipeline without explicit scope and review.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{A controlled refinement loop}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.75em}
\item Owner selects and authorizes one bounded task.
\item Reviewers redact examples and adjudicate a golden dataset.
\item Compare DSPy and smaller-model candidates on held-out cases.
\item Human approves a versioned artifact bundle.
\item Monitor the approved scope and roll back on failure.
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{What the golden dataset contains}
\begin{columns}[T]\column{0.5\textwidth}\begin{block}{Versioned evidence}Representative inputs, expected outcomes, provenance, labeling rules, and edge cases.\end{block}\column{0.5\textwidth}\begin{block}{Evaluation discipline}A held-out split, disagreement adjudication, error categories, and regression checks.\end{block}\end{columns}
\vspace{1em}Refinement means improving labels and coverage under review, not silently collecting more private material.
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Model selection is a measured decision}
Compare baseline and candidate on the same cases. Record model identity, program version, dataset version, quality, latency, and total inference cost.\vspace{1em}
\begin{block}{Promotion gate}A lower-cost candidate advances only when it meets predefined quality and safety thresholds and a human reviewer approves.\end{block}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Possible domains, no live actions}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1em}
\item Internal: support triage, document tagging, task routing.
\item Future research: job discovery, contract research, consulting lead qualification.
\item This public demo performs no ingestion, applications, bids, outreach, contract closing, training, or provider calls.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{What exists today}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1em}
\item React and Storybook illustrate selection, review, simulated evaluation, promotion, and rollback.
\item Playwright tests the UI gates and records a synthetic walkthrough.
\item All sample records and scores are invented; 78\% and 92\% are simulated interface values.
\end{itemize}
